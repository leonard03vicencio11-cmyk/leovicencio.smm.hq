Featured Case Studies

Your Injury Case
Click to read →

Your Injury Case has built a consistent presence across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X with a clear focus on personal injury education and niche practice areas such as catastrophic injuries, workplace accidents, explosions, and electrocution cases. Their branding is cohesive across platforms, and the messaging aligns well with the firm's website, reinforcing trust and legal authority.

The primary opportunity is to improve engagement by evolving beyond text-heavy promotional graphics. Many posts resemble digital flyers, making them difficult to consume in fast-scrolling feeds. A stronger mix of simplified graphics, carousel posts, attorney-led videos, FAQs, client stories, and educational short-form content would create a more engaging experience while positioning Your Injury Case as an accessible legal resource rather than solely a service provider. This shift would help increase organic reach, audience trust, and qualified inquiries.

Golden Rentals
Click to read →

Golden Rentals has built a strong reputation as a trusted, locally owned equipment and party rental company serving Central Pennsylvania since 2004. Their website clearly communicates their wide range of rental equipment, exceptional customer service, and expanding inventory—including trucks, vans, trailers, and specialized equipment—while reinforcing their community-first values. The business is well-positioned within its local market thanks to its comprehensive rental offerings and consistently positive customer feedback.

The biggest opportunity lies in expanding and modernizing their social media presence. Their current digital footprint appears limited, with Facebook serving as the primary social platform while an active public Instagram presence and official LinkedIn company page are not readily identifiable. Creating consistent educational and project-focused content—such as equipment demonstrations, lawn care tips, before-and-after transformations, seasonal project ideas, storm recovery resources, contractor spotlights, and short-form videos—would significantly improve engagement and local brand awareness. Highlighting new equipment arrivals, customer success stories, behind-the-scenes maintenance, and staff expertise would position Golden Rentals as not only an equipment rental provider but also a trusted local resource for homeowners, contractors, landscapers, and event planners. This content strategy would strengthen community engagement, increase organic reach, and generate more rental inquiries throughout the year.

Peace of Mind Pest Control
Click to read →

Peace of Mind Pest Control has established a professional presence across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn that aligns well with its website and overall brand identity. Their social content reflects the company's core values of trust, prevention, and customer service, highlighting residential and commercial pest control, termite services, technician spotlights, community involvement, and educational pest awareness. The branding remains consistent through the use of the company's signature navy blue, red, and white color palette, reinforcing recognition across digital touchpoints. Their LinkedIn profile also reinforces their local expertise, family-oriented approach, and service coverage throughout Stanislaus, San Joaquin, and Merced Counties.

The primary opportunity is to modernize their content strategy by shifting away from predominantly promotional graphics and service announcements toward more engaging, educational, and story-driven content. While the existing posts communicate professionalism, they could generate stronger organic engagement by incorporating simplified infographic-style graphics, seasonal pest prevention tips, carousel posts, behind-the-scenes technician content, customer success stories, FAQs, before-and-after service visuals, and short-form educational videos. A greater emphasis on homeowner education and prevention—rather than only advertising services—would better reflect the company's "Peace of Mind" brand promise, strengthen community trust, and position the business as the go-to local pest control authority while increasing reach, engagement, and qualified leads.

Lori Watson
Click to read →

The Law Office of Lori Watson has established a professional and trustworthy presence that aligns closely with its website and brand identity. Content consistently focuses on educating families about divorce, child custody, child support, estate planning, and other Texas family law matters while maintaining a compassionate and reassuring tone. The firm's messaging emphasizes guidance, practical legal solutions, and decades of experience, positioning Lori Watson as a trusted family law attorney serving Georgetown and Williamson County. Recent posts also reflect current legal topics, such as uncontested divorce, child support updates, and estate planning, demonstrating a commitment to keeping the audience informed.

The primary opportunity for growth is to evolve the visual content strategy. Much of the current content relies on static promotional graphics with text-heavy layouts that communicate information effectively but may not maximize engagement on modern social media platforms. Introducing cleaner infographic-style graphics, carousel posts, attorney spotlight content, short educational videos, FAQ series, and branded illustrations would make legal information easier to consume while creating a more recognizable visual identity. A stronger mix of educational storytelling, attorney-focused branding, and short-form video content would help increase organic reach, strengthen audience trust, and position the Law Office of Lori Watson as one of the leading educational resources for Texas family law in the Georgetown area.

All Reasons Moving & Storage
Click to read →

All Reasons Moving & Storage has established a professional and recognizable presence across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn, with branding that consistently reflects its purple-and-white visual identity, company vehicles, warehouse facilities, and moving crews. Their content focuses on educating customers about moving services, packing tips, long-distance relocations, senior moving assistance, storage solutions, and commercial moves while reinforcing key trust signals such as their Wheaton Van Lines partnership, Silver Certified designation, and more than 30 years of experience. Recent LinkedIn activity also demonstrates community involvement through local events and educational moving content, helping reinforce credibility and authority within the Bay Area moving industry.

The primary opportunity is to improve engagement by shifting from predominantly promotional flyer-style graphics toward more dynamic, people-focused, and educational content. While the branding is consistent, many posts rely heavily on text overlays and service promotions that can blend into crowded social feeds. Incorporating more behind-the-scenes videos, moving myths vs. facts, customer success stories, day-in-the-life content featuring the crew, interactive carousel posts, short-form moving tips, warehouse tours, and testimonial videos would create a stronger connection with prospective customers. A greater emphasis on authentic storytelling and video-first content would better showcase the company's professionalism and customer experience while increasing organic reach, engagement, and qualified inquiries across all platforms.
