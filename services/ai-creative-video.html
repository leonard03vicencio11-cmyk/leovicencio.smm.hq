> 05 // QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Do you create AI-generated video ads and commercials?

Yes. I produce AI-powered commercials and AI-generated short-form video, including the TradeAnchor AI Drama Reels series, alongside traditional short-form ads.

What kind of short-form video do you make?

Reels and short ads for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, such as storm-prep and service ads for local businesses and product videos for SaaS brands.

Do you write the scripts too?

Yes. I handle the hook, script, storyboard, and on-screen text, so the video is built around one clear message and call to action.

Can you design graphics and carousels as well?

Yes. Graphic design, carousels, and ad creative are part of the creative production service, made to match each platform’s format.

Can the videos be used for paid ads?

Yes. Videos and creatives are made in the formats paid campaigns need, and can be produced in variations for testing.

How does a creative project start?

It starts with the project intake form, followed by a short brief on the product, audience, offer, and where the content will run.
