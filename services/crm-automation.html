> 01 // OVERVIEW

Leads don’t go cold on their own. Missing systems let them.

Most businesses already have the tools — a CRM, a calendar, forms, email, SMS. What’s missing is the connection between them. Leads sit in an inbox, follow-up depends on someone remembering, and the same data gets typed into three places. I map how leads and work actually move through your business, then build automations that do the repetitive parts reliably.

> 08 // QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Do you build GoHighLevel workflows?

Yes. I build GoHighLevel pipelines, booking and appointment workflows, lead scoring with conditional logic, and tailored email and SMS follow-up sequences with reminders.

Do you build n8n automations?

Yes. I build n8n workflows for lead enrichment (Apollo.io, LinkedIn, Mail.so, AI summaries), AI assistant agents, and employee onboarding and offboarding across Google Workspace and Slack.

Which CRMs and automation tools do you work with?

GoHighLevel, HubSpot, n8n, Zapier, and Make, plus the tools they connect to, such as Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Google Workspace, Slack, Apollo.io, and Mail.so.

Can you connect my forms, booking calendar, and ads to my CRM?

Yes. Lead capture is the starting point of most systems I build: forms, booking calendars, and ad leads flow into the CRM, get tagged and scored, and trigger the right follow-up automatically.

Do I need to switch to new tools?

Usually not. I build around the tools you already use wherever possible, and only recommend a change when the current setup can’t do what the business needs.

How does a CRM or automation project start?

It starts with the project intake form, followed by a walkthrough of your current process. From there I propose the workflow, the tools, and the scope before anything is built.
