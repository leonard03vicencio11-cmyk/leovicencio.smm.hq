> 05 // HOW I WORK

The Social Photosynthesis Framework

Growth works like a living system. Each stage feeds the next, and the whole thing only works when the roots — your systems — are in place.

  1. 01 SeedThe business begins with clarity: goals, audience, offer, and positioning.
  2. 02 AA BatteryAesthetic and authenticity: a recognizable look and a voice people trust.
  3. 03 RootsThe systems underneath: calendars, workflows, tracking, and follow-up.
  4. 04 FlowerEngagement: content and community that make people stop, react, and reply.
  5. 05 LeadsAttention turns into enquiries through clear calls to action and capture.
  6. 06 SalesLeads are followed up and converted, and the results feed the next cycle.

Visibility → Engagement → Leads → Sales — with CRM, automation, and reporting underneath.

> 08 // QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What does a social media manager do for a business?

A social media manager plans the strategy, creates and schedules content, manages comments and messages, and reports on performance so social media supports real business goals such as awareness, enquiries, and sales. I handle all of these and connect them to lead capture and follow-up when needed.

Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Threads. Each platform gets its own plan, because each one speaks differently and reaches a different audience.

Can a Philippines-based social media manager work with US businesses?

Yes. I work remotely with US and international businesses, including law firms, equipment rental, moving and storage, pest control, and SaaS brands.

How does a social media engagement start?

It starts with the project intake form, followed by a discovery conversation and a review of your current pages, content, and goals. From there I propose a plan sized to what your business actually needs.

How much does social media management cost?

Pricing depends on the platforms, posting volume, and how much creative, community management, and reporting is included. Share your budget range in the intake form and I’ll recommend a scope that fits it.

Can social media be connected to ads, CRM, and automation?

Yes. When growth needs more than posting, I connect social media to paid ads, landing pages, and CRM and automation in GoHighLevel and n8n so new leads are captured and followed up automatically.
