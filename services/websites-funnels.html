> 01 // OVERVIEW

Your website should be your best salesperson.

Most visitors decide in a few seconds whether to stay. If the page is unclear, slow, or has no obvious next step, they leave — and the leads your social media and ads worked to earn are gone. I build sites around one question: what should this visitor do next? Then I make that step easy and make sure the enquiry lands in your CRM.

ERR_02

No clear next step

No obvious call to action, booking link, or form — so interested people leave.

ERR_04

Leads that go nowhere

Form submissions sit in an inbox instead of reaching a CRM with automatic follow-up.

> 08 // QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Do you build websites for small and service businesses?

Yes. I design and build business websites and landing pages for service businesses, law firms, SaaS brands, and communities, including tradeanchor.ai and the EnRICHed Women’s Social Club membership site.

Which platforms do you build on?

WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, and custom-coded sites, depending on what you need to manage and grow the site.

Can you build a lead generation or membership funnel?

Yes. I build landing pages and funnels with clear calls to action, forms, booking, and lead capture, and can connect them to your CRM so every enquiry is followed up.

Will my website be SEO-ready?

Every build includes on-page SEO foundations: page titles, meta descriptions, headings, structured data, sitemap, and mobile performance, so search engines and AI assistants can understand your business.

Can you add tracking like GA4 and Meta Pixel?

Yes. I set up GA4, Google Search Console, Meta Pixel, and UTM tracking so you can see where visitors and leads come from.

How does a website project start?

It starts with the project intake form, followed by a discovery conversation about goals, pages, content, and integrations. From there I propose the structure and scope.
